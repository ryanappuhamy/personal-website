Personal project

An equity research tool for my own investing

I wanted proper research on the stocks I hold without paying for a terminal, so I built it on free, official data: SEC filings, Federal Reserve data and reported financials. It turns a ticker into a research note, and adds a portfolio tracker, a savings plan simulator and alerts.

01 / 08

Research report

Type a ticker and get a research note: price, one-year change, 52-week range and chart.

Numbers from filings and data providers are stored as they are. The written comment is generated separately, labelled, and told never to quote a figure that is not in its input. If no language model is reachable, a fixed template takes over.

02 / 08

Fundamentals and insider trades

Six cards cover valuation, growth, profitability, financial health and the last twelve months of financials.

Insider purchases and sales are read directly from the Form 4 filings at the SEC: name, role, size and date. Earnings guidance is taken from the 8‑K, with the legal boilerplate removed first.

03 / 08

How a lookup works

Every ticker goes through the same seven steps: prices, fundamentals, peer comparison, insider trades, earnings call, analyst and macro data, and the written note.

If a source is missing, that section shows a note instead of breaking the report. Results are cached in Postgres, so a repeated lookup costs nothing.

04 / 08

Portfolio

Value and today’s gain with live prices, a chart against the S&P 500 and other indices, and the list of positions.

It also shows the tax due if everything were sold today, by country. The chart is a backtest of the current holdings, and the page says so.

05 / 08

Portfolio health

Return against the market, risk, diversification and valuation, each explained in one plain sentence.

06 / 08

Savings plan simulator

What a monthly investment would have returned over 15 years of real prices, for index funds or any stock in the portfolio, or a Monte Carlo projection from the 10th to the 90th percentile.

Fees, inflation and capital gains tax by country are included.

07 / 08

Alerts

Rules on price, price-to-earnings, revenue growth or insider filings. Each alert is one line with an OK or Triggered status, checked in the background against the live data.

08 / 08

Macro calendar

The week’s US economic releases (ISM, job openings, payrolls, inflation) with the published value against the forecast: the events the weekly brief flags as near-term risks.

A personal project for educational use, not financial advice. Every number traces back to a primary source; analyst figures are shown with their source.
