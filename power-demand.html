Personal project · Forecasting

Forecasting Swiss electricity demand, a day ahead.

Tomorrow's demand moves tomorrow's power price, and the day-ahead auction closes at noon. I built a model that forecasts Switzerland's hourly load for the next day, and tested it with only the information a trader would have at 12:00.

Swissgrid is clearly better, as it should be: it has weather forecasts for the whole country, metered data, intraday updates and knowledge of large consumers. The model beats the naive forecast in every year.

Data quality

The first result was too good, so I checked the data

The first run said the model beat Swissgrid. Before believing it, I compared every series month by month. Both official sources had problems.

  • Swissgrid's published forecast is a copy of the actual load from Sep 2021 to Dec 2022 (a fake 0% error) and broken from Sep 2023. It is compared only on Jan to Aug 2023
  • A few load hours are corrupted: a value frozen for 10 hours, a December weekday at 40% of normal, summer spikes at winter-peak level
  • A fixed floor ("below 4,200 MW is wrong") removed real summer nights and missed a corrupted day at 4,500 MW. Each hour is judged instead against its own normal: the median of the same hour and weekday, 1 and 2 weeks either side
  • 62 hours removed out of 52,608 (0.12%), each logged with its reason
Realistic setup

Only what is known at noon the day before

A forecast that quietly uses information from the future looks better than it is. Two corrections made the test honest, at a measured cost.

  • Load: the latest hour used is 10:00 on the day before. "Same hour yesterday" is gone: at noon, yesterday's evening has not happened yet
  • Weather: trained on observed temperature, tested on the archived forecast made about 48 hours earlier, as in production
  • Surprise: switching to weather forecasts barely moved the error. Recent load already carries most of the weather signal

Method

Retrained every month, scored on the next

Walk-forward validation: train on everything before a month, forecast every hour of that month, move one month forward. 45 folds cover every season and never use the future to predict the past.

Limitations

What this model does not know

  • One city's temperature stands in for the whole country
  • The Swissgrid comparison covers eight months only
  • Error rises from 2023 for every method, possibly a change in how the load is reported
  • No embedded solar, prices or industrial calendar
